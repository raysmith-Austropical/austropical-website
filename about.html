About Austropical

Açaí and tropical fruit sorbet, made in the Brazilian Amazon, where açaí grows. Coming to US freezers in March 2027.

Our story

Started in Australia. Made in the Amazon.

Austropical started in Australia in 2018, when Tarcísio Carvalho founded the company.

Every flavor is made in the Brazilian Amazon, where açaí grows, and fruit is the first ingredient in every one. It comes out of the freezer ready to eat: no blender, just a spoon.

Austropical LLC brings the sorbets to the United States. The US business is led by Ray Smith, a US Navy veteran, from Bayfield, Colorado.

Original Sensation and Mango reach US freezers first, in March 2027. The rest of the line follows.

The Austropical café

Open seven days. Since 2026.

The Austropical café opened in Sydney in 2026, beside a car wash. It serves açaí bowls, smoothies, coffee and desserts seven days a week, and the açaí is Austropical.

The range

Ten flavors, three formats

Ten flavors, each in a pint and in a Grab'n Go cup with its own wooden spoon. Three are USDA Organic: Original Sensation, Mango and Coconut. All ten are certified vegan (Vegan Australia). Açaí Zero Sugar is not a low calorie food.
