From the Amazon to US freezers

About Austropical

Açaí and tropical fruit sorbets made in Castanhal, Pará, Brazil, sold in Australia since 2018, and coming to the United States in March 2027.

The US story

Why I'm bringing Austropical to the US

I learned to work in the US Navy. From 2000 to 2005 I was a nuclear machinist's mate, and my service included Operation Enduring Freedom. In that world you don't guess. You check it, you write it down, and you do it the same way every time.

I carried that into ten years as a field service technician, keeping equipment running for people who needed it working that day.

In July 2023 I started Austropical's US business. Austropical began in Australia, where Tarcísio Carvalho founded it and where it has sold açaí and tropical fruit sorbets through foodservice since 2018. The sorbets are made by Xingu Fruit in Castanhal, in the Brazilian state of Pará.

I started knocking on buyers' doors that year. Looking back, I was about two years too soon. So I spent the time building the part most people skip: all 60 of our items are published to GDSN, our foodservice buckets are set up at Vistar, and this website only says what a label, a certificate or a record can back up.

Right now the US team is me, working from Bayfield, Colorado. Our first container is planned to land at the Port of Newark in February 2027, and the first flavors reach US freezers in March.

If you run a dining hall, a café or a freezer aisle, I'd like to hear from you.

Ray Smith
President, North America, Austropical LLC

Made in Pará

Xingu Fruit, Castanhal, Pará, Brazil

Every Austropical sorbet is made by one producer, Xingu Fruit, in Castanhal in the state of Pará, Brazil, with Amazonian açaí and tropical fruit. The açaí recipes carry 40 to 60% açaí; fruit is first on every label.

The sorbets are pasteurized, then frozen. The facility is FSSC 22000 certified and a USDA NOP certified organic operation (Ecocert).

Since 2018

Austropical Pty Ltd, Australia

Austropical is an Australian company. Austropical Pty Ltd has sold the sorbets through foodservice in Australia since 2018, and holds the brand's USDA organic certification for Original Sensation, Mango and Coconut (NOP ID 3950001101).

The range

Ten flavors, three formats

Açaí and tropical fruit sorbet made in Brazil, coming to US freezers in March 2027. Three flavors are USDA certified organic: Original Sensation, Mango and Coconut. All ten are certified vegan (Vegan Australia). Açaí Zero Sugar is not a low calorie food.

Four açaí sorbets

  • Organic Açaí Original Sensation
  • Açaí Banana Sunrise
  • Açaí Strawberry Crush
  • Açaí Zero Sugar (Not a low calorie food.)

Six tropical fruit sorbets

  • Organic Coconut
  • Organic Mango
  • Passionfruit
  • Dragon Fruit & Pineapple
  • Pink Guava & Acerola
  • Cupuaçu & Coconut

Three formats

  • 500 mL pint (16.9 fl oz)
  • 250 mL Grab'n Go cup with wooden spoon
  • 10.2 kg foodservice bucket
