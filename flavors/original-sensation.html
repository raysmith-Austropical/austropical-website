Organic Açaí Original Sensation

60% organic açaí

Açaí is the small purple fruit of a palm that grows in the Amazon. Organic açaí is 60% of Original Sensation and the first ingredient on the label.

Formats

Certifications

Made in the Brazilian Amazon. Product of Brazil.

Ingredients

Organic Açaí (60%), Filtered Water, Organic Cane Sugar, Organic Agave Syrup, Stabilizer (Sodium Alginate), Natural Flavor, Acidity Regulator (Citric Acid).
