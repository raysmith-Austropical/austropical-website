Açaí Banana Sunrise

Açaí Banana Sunrise is an açaí sorbet with banana: 40% açaí, filtered water, cane sugar and 6% banana, made in the Brazilian Amazon. It is certified vegan (Vegan Australia) and kosher (Kosher Australia). Scoop it as the base of an açaí bowl, or eat it straight from the pint.

What's in it

40% açaí, 6% banana

Açaí, the small purple fruit of a palm that grows in the Amazon, is 40% of Banana Sunrise and the first ingredient on the label. Banana is 6%.

The full ingredient statement

Açaí sorbet with banana: 40% açaí, 6% banana

Ingredients

Organic Açaí (40%), Filtered Water, Cane Sugar, Banana (6%), Stabilizers (Locust Bean Gum, Guar Gum), Natural Flavor, Acidity Regulator (Citric Acid).

No allergen statement is printed or required for this product.

Storage

Keep frozen at 0 °F (−18 °C) or below. Do not let the product thaw and refreeze: temperature changes affect texture and shelf life. Keep the container tightly sealed and store it in the coldest part of the freezer, away from the door. The best-before date is on the container.

Shelf life: 24 months from manufacture, sealed and stored frozen.
