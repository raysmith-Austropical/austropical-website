Açaí Strawberry Crush

Açaí with a crush of strawberry. Made in the Brazilian Amazon, where açaí grows. Scoop it as the base of an açaí bowl, or eat it straight from the pint. Follows Original Sensation and Mango into US freezers.

Ingredients

Organic Açaí (40%), Filtered Water, Cane Sugar, Strawberry (6%), Stabilizers (Xanthan Gum, Locust Bean Gum, Carrageenan), Natural Flavor, Acidity Regulator (Citric Acid).

Storage
