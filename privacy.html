Privacy statement

Plain words, because there is not much to say: this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

What this site collects

Nothing on its own. austropical.co is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms. The pages, images and videos are served from our web host, which keeps ordinary server logs (the address a request came from, the page requested, the time) for security.

The two typefaces on the site, Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans, are served from austropical.co itself, like the pictures and videos, so opening a page sends no request to any other company. Links to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and our Australian site take you to those sites, which have their own privacy terms.

When you write to us

The only way to send us anything is email. When you write to info@austropical.co or raysmith@austropical.co, we keep your email and what you wrote so we can reply. If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address and ZIP code to tell you where Austropical is sold first. We do not sell or share either.

Marketing email

Every marketing email we send carries our postal address and an unsubscribe link, as the CAN-SPAM Act requires. Reply "unsubscribe" or use the link, and we stop. We do not send text messages.

Children

The site is for adults and for buyers in the trade. We do not knowingly collect anything from children.

Changes

If we add analytics or a sign-up form, this page will say so first, with the date at the top updated.
