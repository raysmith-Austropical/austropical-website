Building an açaí bowl program for a campus or café: 69 bowls a bucket, no blender

A 10.2 kg bucket of açaí sorbet gives about 69 bowls at 2/3 cup, with no blender. The four-bowl menu, the Operator Kit, and the Vistar item numbers to order.

An açaí bowl program usually means a blender, a mix recipe, a trained person and a line. Ours means a bucket, a scoop and a topping bar. This post lays out the portion math, the menu, the Operator Kit and how to order. Ask us for the spec sheets.

How many açaí bowls do you get from one bucket?

About 69. The foodservice bucket holds 10.2 kg (22.49 lb) of sorbet. The portion standard is 2/3 cup (147 g), the serving size declared for the bucket. Divide 10.2 kg by 147 g and you get about 69 bowls, before toppings. That is the only portion math we publish.

Do you need a blender for an açaí bowl program?

No. No blender, no mix, no machine to calibrate: a scoop, a freezer, and a topping bar your customers set the value on. The sorbet arrives churned, pasteurized and frozen. Staff scoop 2/3 cup into a cup, add toppings and hand it over. Scoop, top, serve.

Training is a 15-minute sheet. Any student worker can run the counter after reading it.

What is on the four-bowl menu?

One size, one scoop, four flavors:

  • Organic Açaí Original Sensation, the flagship açaí. 60% açaí.
  • Açaí Banana Sunrise, the crowd açaí. 40% açaí with 6% banana.
  • Dragon Fruit & Pineapple, the visual tropical bowl. 50% dragon fruit and 20% pineapple.
  • Pink Guava & Acerola, the rosy one. 29% pink guava and 28% acerola.

Split-flavor comes at no extra charge: half açaí, half dragon fruit in the same cup. The operator owns the menu, so swap in any flavor from the range. All ten flavors come in the same bucket, to the same spec.

What toppings go on the bowls?

Four, and any café already stocks them: granola, banana, strawberry and coconut. The topping bar is yours. You buy the toppings, you decide what goes on, and your customers decide how much a bowl is worth to them.

What is in the Operator Kit?

  • Buckets, 10.2 kg, through Vistar, with the 2/3-cup scoop. About 69 bowls a bucket.
  • A topping bar you already stock: granola, banana, strawberry, coconut.
  • Printed Austropical bowl cups and spoons.
  • Menu board, counter signage and window clings.
  • A 15-minute staff training sheet.
  • A counter or cart: a 4-foot build-out inside an existing café, or a cart.

Together this is the Austropical Açaí Bar: a branded counter or cart inside your existing dining or retail space. You run it and you own the menu. Buckets and cups are bought through Vistar.

Grab'n Go cups (250 mL, wooden spoon included) go into the same campus c-stores, grab-and-go coolers and micro-markets on the same Vistar delivery. Product data for all 60 items, cups, pints and buckets, is published through GDSN.

What are the bucket specs?

  • White square pail with a snap lid; one printed label on the front face.
  • Net 10.2 kg (22.49 lb); gross 10.8 kg (23.53 lb).
  • 24.5 x 29.5 x 24.8 cm (9.65 x 11.61 x 9.76 in).
  • Sold by the bucket, not cased. 120 buckets per pallet, 15 per layer by 8 layers, stretch-wrapped.
  • Shelf life 24 months from manufacture, sealed and stored frozen.
  • Keep frozen at 0 °F (−18 °C) or below.
  • Made in the Brazilian Amazon, in an FSSC 22000 certified facility.

The US program is built on one idea: one bucket, one scoop, and a menu the operator owns.
