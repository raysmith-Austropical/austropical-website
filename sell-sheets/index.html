What is on a sheet

Built for the buyer and the item set-up

Page one

The flavor and what it tastes like, the fruit share, the three formats with their sizes and case packs, why buyers pick it, and how to reach Ray, with a code that opens the request form.

Page two

The specification: net quantity, weights, GTINs with scannable EAN-13 and ITF-14 barcodes, unit and case dimensions, pallet Ti × Hi, Vistar item, shelf life and storage, ingredients, Nutrition Facts and certifications.

The same data in GDSN

All 60 items are published through Syndigo (information provider GLN 9377779574331), so your systems can pull them. The buckets are set up at Vistar, items TLL65266 to TLL65275.
