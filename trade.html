Certifications and origin

What is certified

USDA Organic

Original Sensation, Mango and Coconut. Austropical Pty Ltd, NOP Operation ID 3950001101, status Certified on the USDA Organic Integrity Database.

Vegan and kosher

All ten flavors are certified vegan (Vegan Australia). Nine are kosher (Kosher Australia); Strawberry Crush is not.

Facility and origin

Made by Xingu Fruit, Castanhal, Pará, Brazil, in an FSSC 22000 certified facility; a USDA NOP certified organic operation (Ecocert). Pasteurized. Product of Brazil. No allergen statement is printed or required.

Ingredient statements and the declared Nutrition Facts per 2/3 cup (147 g) are on each flavor page. Açaí Zero Sugar: Not a low calorie food.
