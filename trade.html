Pallet

Pints and cups: 144 cases a pallet, 16 a layer × 9 layers; case 12.05 × 7.99 × 9.06 in, 12.43 lb gross for pints (12.30 lb on the three 420 g flavors) and 10.10 lb for cups. Buckets: 120 a pallet, 15 a layer × 8 layers, on a 47.24 × 39.37 in (1200 × 1000 mm) pallet, loaded height 83.82 in, stretch-wrapped.

Shelf life and storage

24 months from manufacture, sealed and stored frozen. Keep frozen at 0 °F (−18 °C) or below. Once opened, consume the same day.

Certifications and origin

What is certified

USDA Organic

Original Sensation, Mango and Coconut. Austropical Pty Ltd, NOP Operation ID 3950001101, status Certified on the USDA Organic Integrity Database.

Vegan and kosher

All ten flavors are certified vegan (Vegan Australia). Nine are kosher (Kosher Australia); Strawberry Crush is not.

Facility and origin

Made in the Brazilian Amazon, in an FSSC 22000 certified facility that is a USDA NOP certified organic operation. Facility certificates come with our supplier paperwork. Pasteurized. Product of Brazil. No allergen statement is printed or required.

Ingredient statements and the declared Nutrition Facts per 2/3 cup (147 g) are on each flavor page. Açaí Zero Sugar: Not a low calorie food.
