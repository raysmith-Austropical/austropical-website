Ten flavors

Açaí sorbet and tropical fruit sorbet: ten flavors

Açaí and tropical fruit sorbet made in Brazil, coming to US freezers in March 2027. Three flavors are USDA certified organic: Original Sensation, Mango and Coconut. All ten are certified vegan (Vegan Australia). Açaí Zero Sugar is not a low calorie food.

  • Organic Açaí Original Sensation Açaí sorbet, 60% açaí. USDA Organic.
  • Açaí Banana Sunrise Açaí sorbet with banana.
  • Açaí Strawberry Crush Açaí sorbet with strawberry.
  • Açaí Zero Sugar Açaí sorbet. Not a low calorie food.
  • Organic Coconut Coconut sorbet, 45% coconut. USDA Organic.
  • Organic Mango Mango sorbet, 60% mango. USDA Organic.
  • Passionfruit Passion fruit sorbet, 30% passionfruit.
  • Dragon Fruit & Pineapple 50% dragon fruit, 20% pineapple.
  • Pink Guava & Acerola Rosy pink guava and acerola sorbet.
  • Cupuaçu & Coconut Cupuaçu sorbet with coconut.

Three formats

Pint, Grab'n Go cup, foodservice bucket

  • 500 mL pint

    16.9 fl oz. Paper tub, foil seal, paper lid. About three servings. Twelve pints to a case.

  • 250 mL Grab'n Go cup

    One serving, wooden spoon included. For grab-and-go coolers, c-stores and micro-markets. Eighteen cups to a case.

  • 10.2 kg foodservice bucket

    About 69 bowls at a 2/3 cup (147 g) scoop. Ten flavors, one spec. Set up at Vistar.
