From the Amazon

Made in the Amazon. Sold in Australia since 2018. Coming to the US.

Austropical sorbets are made in the Brazilian Amazon from Amazonian açaí and tropical fruit. Austropical Pty Ltd has sold them through foodservice in Australia since 2018. Austropical LLC, of Bayfield, Colorado, brings them to the United States.

  • The açaí recipes carry 40 to 60% açaí; fruit is first on every label.
  • Pasteurized, then frozen.
  • Three flavors USDA certified organic; all ten certified vegan (Vegan Australia).
About Austropical

The bowl at home

No blender. Just a scoop.

A 2/3 cup scoop of sorbet is the base of the bowl; the toppings are yours.

  1. 01

    Scoop

    2/3 cup (147 g) of sorbet from the pint, straight from the freezer. No blender.

  2. 02

    Top

    Granola, banana, strawberry, coconut. As much of each as you like.

  3. 03

    Eat

    Frozen, right away. The pint goes back in the freezer at 0 °F (−18 °C) or below.
