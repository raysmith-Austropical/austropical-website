Ten flavors

Açaí sorbet and tropical fruit sorbet: ten flavors

Açaí and tropical fruit sorbet made in Brazil, coming to US freezers in March 2027. Three flavors are USDA certified organic: Original Sensation, Mango and Coconut. All ten are certified vegan (Vegan Australia). Açaí Zero Sugar is not a low calorie food.

From the Amazon

Made in the Amazon. Sold in Australia since 2018. Coming to the US.

Austropical sorbets are made in the Brazilian Amazon from Amazonian açaí and tropical fruit. Austropical Pty Ltd has sold them through foodservice in Australia since 2018. Austropical LLC, of Bayfield, Colorado, brings them to the United States.

  • The açaí recipes carry 40 to 60% açaí; fruit is first on every label.
  • Pasteurized, then frozen.
  • Three flavors USDA certified organic; all ten certified vegan (Vegan Australia).

Serving suggestions

Scoop, top and serve

A 2/3 cup scoop of sorbet is the base of the bowl. No blender: the toppings are yours.

  • Fruit on top

    Banana, strawberry, blueberries, kiwi and coconut over a scoop of açaí.

    Serving suggestion.

  • Berries and granola

    Raspberries, blueberries and a handful of granola, in a coconut shell bowl.

    Serving suggestion.

  • Toppings on the side

    Set out granola, nuts and chopped fruit and let everyone build their own bowl.

    Serving suggestion.

The bowl program

Three formats

Pint, Grab'n Go cup, foodservice bucket

  • 500 mL pint

    16.9 fl oz. Paper tub, foil seal, paper lid. About three servings. Twelve pints to a case.

  • 250 mL Grab'n Go cup

    One serving, wooden spoon included. For grab-and-go coolers, c-stores and micro-markets. Eighteen cups to a case.

  • 10.2 kg foodservice bucket

    About 69 bowls at a 2/3 cup (147 g) scoop. Ten flavors, one spec. Set up at Vistar.
