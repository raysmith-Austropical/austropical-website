Inside the caféBring it to your counter

The café serves açaí as soft serve. In the US, Austropical is a sorbet: in pints, Grab’n Go cups and foodservice buckets.

  1. Made where açaí grows

    From the Brazilian Amazon.

    Açaí grows on palms in the Brazilian Amazon. Our sorbet is made right there, then travels frozen all the way to your freezer.

  2. Fruit first

    Fruit comes first.

    Fruit is the first ingredient in every flavor. Original Sensation and Zero Sugar are mostly açaí. Zero Sugar is not a low calorie food.

  3. March 2027

    Coming to US freezers.

    Original Sensation and Mango arrive first; the rest of the line follows. Leave your ZIP code and we'll tell you where.

Our story
