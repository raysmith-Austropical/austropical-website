Distributors

What a distributor gets today

Everything an item set-up needs is public. Pricing, terms and the bucket program's allowances come from Ray by email.

  • A sell sheet for every GTIN. All 60 items (ten flavors in pints, pint cases, cups, cup cases, buckets and bucket pallets) are published in GDSN through Syndigo; each code in the identifiers table opens its sell sheet as a PDF with ingredients, Nutrition Facts, dimensions, case and pallet data.
  • All 60 GTINs in one file. Download the GTIN list (CSV): flavor, item, GTIN-14, sell-sheet link and Vistar item number.
  • Already set up. Vistar (Performance Food Group), supplier number 101808, buckets TLL65266 to TLL65275; Shamrock Foods vendor set-up; information provider GLN 9377779574331.
  • Pack renders and 360 videos for your catalog and item images: the press kit.
  • Certificates by email: the USDA Organic certificate (also on the public USDA Organic Integrity Database, operation 3950001101), Vegan Australia, Kosher Australia, and the facility's FSSC 22000.
  • Pallet configuration drawings for the bucket pallets, by email.

  • Shelf facts for your planogram: the pint and the cup share a 3.82-inch footprint; sizes, case packs and GTINs are below and in the identifiers table, where every code opens its sell sheet (PDF).
  • Pack images for listings and planograms: front-of-pack renders of every pint and cup, and the 360 videos, in the press kit.
  • Distribution. Set up at Vistar (Performance Food Group) and Shamrock Foods; product data live in GDSN for all 60 items, so your item set-up can pull from the source.
  • The flavors, with ingredients and the declared Nutrition Facts, on the flavor pages. Açaí Zero Sugar is not a low calorie food.
  • Samples when stock lands in March 2027.

Foodservice operators

What an operator gets today

The açaí bowl program runs on a 10.2 kg bucket, a scoop and the toppings you already stock. The Grab'n Go cup goes in the same coolers, c-stores and micro-markets on the same delivery.

  • The bowl program. A 2/3-cup (147 g) scoop gives about 69 bowls a bucket before toppings. No blender, no mix, no machine to calibrate: scoop, top, serve. The program, the Operator Kit and the four-bowl menu are on the foodservice page.
  • Order through Vistar. The ten buckets are set up at Vistar (Performance Food Group), items TLL65266 to TLL65275, supplier 101808. Not a Vistar customer yet? Vistar's Become a Customer page is the start.
  • The bucket spec and the pallet drawing: the spec block and the sell sheet for each bucket in the identifiers table.
  • Grab'n Go cups for coolers, c-stores and micro-markets: 250 mL, with a wooden spoon, 18 a case.
  • Samples when stock lands in March 2027, and a call about a bowl counter in your space.
