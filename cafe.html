Made to order

Four moves. Every bowl.

At the counter, each bowl is poured, swirled, topped and handed over while you watch.

01 · Pour

Açaí, poured to order.

It comes out of the machine cold and thick, straight into the cup.

02 · Swirl

Swirled tall, by hand.

Açaí on its own, or swirled with a second flavor, built up the cup in one go.

03 · Top

Sauce over the top.

Pistachio, caramel or chocolate sauce, poured over the top.

04 · Serve

Fruit, a spoon, and it is yours.

Banana, strawberry, granola and a wooden spoon. On the tray and out to the table.

For US campuses, cafés and venues

Bring the counter home: the Austropical Açaí Bar.

No blender and no soft-serve machine. Our 22.49 lb bucket of açaí sorbet scoops straight into the bowl: about 69 bowls a bucket at a 2/3-cup scoop.

The Açaí Bar is an Austropical counter or cart inside your café, campus market or venue. You run it and keep the bowl margin. There is no license fee, and the buckets come through Vistar.

  • Buckets of açaí sorbet, ordered through Vistar
  • Printed Austropical bowl cups and spoons
  • A menu board, counter signage and window clings
  • A 15-minute training sheet for your staff
  • A counter build-out or a cart
