Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to read about Austropical and reach us, whatever they use to browse.

What we aim for

austropical.co is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. Every page is checked with automated tests before it is published, at phone and desktop widths, and the forms, menus and the 360 viewer are built to work with a keyboard and a screen reader.

What that means on the site

  • Text and background colors meet the AA contrast ratio, including on the colored flavor pages.
  • Every image that carries meaning has a text description; decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
  • The site works without JavaScript: forms fall back to an email, and the 360 viewers show a still.
  • Motion respects the "reduce motion" setting on your device.
  • Headings, landmarks and a skip link make each page navigable by keyboard.
  • Videos are silent and carry on-screen text; where a video has words, they are also in the page.

Tell us if something does not work

If any part of this site is hard to use, email info@austropical.co or call 970-317-9458 and say which page and what happened. We answer within five business days and fix what we can.
